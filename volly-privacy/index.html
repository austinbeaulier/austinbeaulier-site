Volly Tennis Club

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Volly is a tennis game by Austin Beaulier. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never asks for your name, email or other contact details. The short version: nothing you do in Volly leaves your phone.

The camera

Volly uses your iPhone's front camera to see your swings. Each video frame is analyzed on your phone, using Apple's built-in Vision framework, to find where your shoulders, elbows, wrists and hips are. Then the frame is thrown away.

  • Video and photos are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. We never see them.
  • The small camera preview during a match is only shown on your phone.
  • You can play without the camera using touch controls, and you can turn off camera access any time in the Settings app.

Data that stays on your phone

Your settings, such as match type, difficulty, racket hand, controls, swing sensitivity and whether you've read the safety notice, are saved on your phone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. Volly doesn't collect, store or share personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Apple

Downloads and updates go through the App Store. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. Apple's privacy policy covers this.

Children

Volly is suitable for all ages. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
