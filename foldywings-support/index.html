Help and support

Need help? Email austinbeaulier@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I fly?

On iPhone Duo, fold the phone to flap, hold it open to glide, and tilt it left or right to turn. On any other iPhone, tap to flap and tilt to turn.

Why did my flight end?

You crashed into the ground, a tree or a building, or you ran out of energy and fell. Fly through rings to refill your energy.

I missed a day. Can I get my streak back?

A missed day restarts your streak at day 1. Your coins and birds are safe.

Why doesn't the leaderboard open?

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then try again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tap the gear button on the title screen. To control tracking, use the Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Read more in our privacy policy.
