How the options fit together

  • Stacking lip Lets another bin sit on top. The bin above clicks into the rim, so it can't slide off. The bin on top must cover the same grid squares as the bins below it: one bin the same size, or a few small bins it covers exactly. Nothing can be placed on top of a bin without a stacking lip.
  • Magnet holes Optional. Snaps to a magnet baseplate. Magnets work in pairs: one in each pocket under the bin, one in the baseplate pocket below it. There are 4 pockets per grid square, so a 2 × 3 bin takes 24 magnets, and a magnet baseplate takes 4 per square too. The parts list counts them all for you. On an open baseplate these bins still fit, they just don't snap down.
  • Label tab A shelf for a label, along the wall you pick (the back, unless you change it). It doesn't change how the bin fits.
  • Dividers Split the bin into compartments. They don't change how the bin fits either.
  • Baseplate Lies in your drawer. Every bin's feet click into its grid squares, whatever options the bin has.
