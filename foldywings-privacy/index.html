Foldy Wings

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Foldy Wings is a game by Austin Beaulier. It has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email or other contact details. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

Data that stays on your phone

Your coins, best time, birds, streak, missions and a log of game events (such as when a flight ends or an ad is shown) are saved on your phone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Foldy Wings shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • a device identifier, such as your phone's advertising identifier
  • which ads you have seen and how you interact with them and the app
  • crash logs and performance data, such as launch time and battery use

Google uses this data for advertising and analytics, and may share it with other companies that display ads. Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and its page on how it uses data from partner apps.

We do not receive this data about you as an individual. AdMob gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices about ads

  • Ad tracking: iOS asks whether Foldy Wings may track you. If you say no, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, including the EEA, the UK and some US states, Google's consent form appears in the app. You can change your choice any time from the gear button on the title screen, under Ad privacy choices.

Apple services

Leaderboard scores go through Game Center. Apple's privacy policy covers these services.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a reminder on your phone when your daily reward is ready. No data leaves your phone to do this. You can turn reminders off in the Settings app.

Children

Foldy Wings is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to those rated suitable for general audiences.

Keeping and deleting data

We don't hold data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove what's on your phone, delete the app. To reset your advertising identifier, or limit its use, use the Settings app. For data Google holds, see Google's privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
