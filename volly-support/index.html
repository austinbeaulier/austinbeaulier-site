How do I set up?

Prop your iPhone up sideways at about chest height, with the screen facing you. Step back 2–3 m (7–10 ft) so your upper body and both arms fit in the picture. The setup screen ticks off each step, then you swing to start.

My swings aren't registering

  • Play somewhere well lit, and keep your shoulders, elbows and hands in the picture.
  • Swing through the ball with your whole arm, not just your wrist.
  • Pause the game and slide Swing sensitivity toward the hare for gentler swings. If random movements count as swings, slide it toward the tortoise.

How do I serve?

Raise a hand above your head to toss the ball, then swing as it comes down.

How do I aim?

Timing aims the ball. Swing a little early to pull it cross-court, a little late to send it down the line. Swing harder for more power.

Can two people play?

Yes. Choose Match → 2 Players and stand side by side, at least an arm's length apart. Whoever is on the left is Player 1. Each of you only swings for your own player.

Can I play without the camera?

Yes. Choose Controls → Touch, then swipe to swing and tap for a medium shot.

Safety and terms

Volly is a physical game. Before you play:

  • Clear the area. Leave room to swing in every direction, and move people, pets, furniture, lamps and anything breakable out of reach. Watch for ceiling fans and low ceilings.
  • Play on a flat floor that isn't slippery, in shoes or bare feet, not socks.
  • Prop your phone up securely where it can't fall, and don't hold it while you swing.
  • Warm up and take breaks. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, and ask a doctor first if you have a health condition that physical activity could affect.
  • Children should play with an adult nearby.
